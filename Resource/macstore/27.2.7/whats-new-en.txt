Make and refine widgets by talking to AI.

• AI is now a conversation: describe a widget, look at the preview, ask for a change, and go back to any earlier version. Nothing changes until you save or apply it.
• Each change shows the lines it added and removed, and the result is run on your device before you see it.
• The editor's Copilot is the same conversation, about the widget you have open.
• Anthropic is available as an AI provider, next to Apple Private Cloud Compute, OpenAI-compatible servers and Ollama.
• Widgets written for Android now show on iPhone, iPad and Mac: lists draw the rows that fit, and wallpaper colors use a fixed palette.
• Every bundled template is explained with comments and fits its text at each size.
• Safer AI connections, a fix for scripts that end with a comment, and layout fixes in the editor.

AI features use the provider chosen in Settings. Some templates require device permissions or an internet connection.
